studio ez is a Dhaka-based creative studio working across brand identity and strategy, UX/UI, gamification, motion and video, and content production.

We have delivered an interactive learning experience for the Income Tax Division of the National Board of Revenue, built brand and content for F-commerce businesses, produced a podcast end to end, and worked on many more projects for overseas clients.

We take a project from written brief to a finished, working deliverable, including projects where design, animation and code have to come together.
